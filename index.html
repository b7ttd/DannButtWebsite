Resume and Experience

Dann Butt is a technical theater professional, project coordinator, and lifelong builder from Long Island, New York. He is currently pursuing a B.A. in Environmental Studies with a minor in Theatre at Fordham University, where he discovered his passion for production while working in the scene shop.

Dann's work lives at the intersection of craft, logistics, and creative collaboration. From scene shops in the Bronx to the grand stage of the Metropolitan Opera, he has learned that the best productions are built on precision, communication, and a little bit of grit. He has worked on everything from Shakespearean classics to grand opera, helping bring ambitious designs to life.

Currently, Dann splits his time between Fordham Theater Company (as a shop carpenter/painter), The Metropolitan Opera, and American Ballet Theatre. When not backstage, he enjoys running trails, volunteering with event operations, and planning his next build project.

Dann believes in honest work, clear communication, and the power of collaboration. Whether it's a sprawling opera set or a tight-turnaround event, he shows up ready to solve problems and support the team.

The Tempest

Role: Shop Carpenter & Painter

Year: 2025

Venue: Fordham Theater Company

Director: Ryan Quinn

About the Production

By William Shakespeare

The Tempest tells the story of Prospero, the rightful Duke of Milan, who has been exiled to a remote island with his daughter Miranda after being overthrown by his brother Antonio.

Cast

Principal Cast: Carlos Bravo (Prospero), Isabelle Diaz (Miranda), Dylan Stern (Caliban)

Ariel Ensemble: Isabella Conklin, Carrie Johnson, Jilliyn Acosta, Brian Tong

Supporting Cast: Leah John (Ferdinand), Brian Muñoz Reyes (Stephano), Tobias Foreman (Trinculo), Fiona Nealis (Alonza), Avery Ash (Sebastian), Kei Sugae (Antonio), Maia McGowan (Gonzalo)

Production Team

Director: Ryan Quinn | Assistant Director: Elias Bernstein

Stage Manager: Adam Taylor Foster | ASM: Teresa Ayers, Bel Lazarsky

Scenic Designer: Brittany Vasta | Lighting Designer: Lucrecia Briceño | Sound Designer: Elijah Warrington

Costume Designer: Harri Horsley | Props Designer: Deb Gaouette

My Role

As Shop Carpenter & Painter, I contributed to the construction and finishing of scenic elements for this production. Worked closely with the technical director and scenic designer to bring the vision to life, ensuring all set pieces met design specifications and safety standards.
