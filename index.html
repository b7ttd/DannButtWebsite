Dann Butt is a audio-visual fanatic, project coordinator, and lifelong theater kid from Long Island, New York. He is currently pursuing a B.A. in Environmental Studies with a minor in Theatre at Fordham University.

Dann's work lives at the intersection of sustainability, logistics, and creative collaboration. From lifeguarding on Long Beach to the making stage premieres at Metropolitan Opera, he has been fortunate to have many career opportunities and life experiences that have molded him into the go-getter and leader that he has become today. While he admits he has much to learn, he strives to create productions built on precision, communication, and grit.

Currently, Dann splits his time between being a full-time student, and the many jobs and passion projects he continues to engage himself with. When not backstage, he enjoys running trails, desinging websites, and planning his next build project.

Dann believes in honest work, clear communication, and the power of collaboration.
