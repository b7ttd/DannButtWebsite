About Kindeva

Kindeva is a leading Contract Development and Manufacturing Organization (CDMO) specializing in complex drug delivery systems. The company holds the distinction of being the first to patent the inhaler, pioneering respiratory drug delivery technology that has transformed how patients receive life-saving medications for asthma, COPD, and other respiratory conditions.

With decades of innovation in drug-device combination products, Kindeva continues to lead the industry in developing and manufacturing inhaled therapies, transdermal patches, and other advanced drug delivery systems.

Marketing Activation & Brand Ambassadorship

We hit the streets of Manhattan again for another DCAT Week Conference, this time for the @kindevadd brand.

Wearing Kindeva orange, our brand ambassadors handed out sweet treats and memorable swag, from cake pops and custom branded mint candy to inhaler-shaped stress balls - a clever nod to Kindeva's pioneering history as the first to patent the inhaler.

Plus we rolled three LED billboard trucks through high traffic areas, pumping music and good vibes to everyone we passed. This mobile marketing approach created a dynamic, eye-catching presence throughout Manhattan, ensuring Kindeva's brand message reached far beyond the convention center walls.
